I am a PhD student in Mathematics at the University of Cambridge, working in probability theory. I am a Gates Cambridge Scholar, and my supervisor is [Professor Wendelin Werner](https://www.dpmms.cam.ac.uk/~ww295/).

My current interests include percolation, random walks, cover and meeting times, the Gaussian free field, and Brownian loop soups.
