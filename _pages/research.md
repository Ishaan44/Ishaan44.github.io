My research is in probability theory, especially percolation, random walks, cover and meeting times, Gaussian free fields, and loop soups.

## Papers and preprints

### Percolation

- **Critical Threshold for Regular Graphs**  
  Ishaan Bhadoo.  
  [[arXiv:2412.00635]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.00635)

### Random walks and dynamical percolation

- **Meeting times and cover times in dynamical percolation**  
  In progress.  
  Comparison principles for vertex-transitive graphs and conditions for tightness of Matthews-type bounds in dynamical settings.

### Trees and word percolation

- **Percolation of words**  
  With Ritvik Radhakrishnan.  
  Generalises the Kesten--Benjamini problem for locally finite trees.  
  [[PDF]](/assets/pdf/Tree_Proof.pdf) · [[Progress notes]](/assets/pdf/Percolation_of_Words.pdf)
